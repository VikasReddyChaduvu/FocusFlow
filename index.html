How to Use FocusFlow

Master the Calendar Ideology, streak preservation, and daily planning.

What is FocusFlow?

FocusFlow is built on the Calendar Ideology: rather than treating habits as isolated checkboxes, every single habit is laid out horizontally across the month. Seeing your chain of unbroken days builds undeniable psychological momentum.

1

Create Your Habits

Navigate to the Habits tab, click "+ Create New Habit", choose a category, and assign a priority level to determine XP gains.

2

Mark Daily Progress

On the Dashboard Calendar Matrix, click any square for today or past days to tick it off and build your chain.

3

Defend Your Streak

Earn streak badges, level up your XP, and engage Focus Mode during deep work sessions using the Pomodoro timer.
